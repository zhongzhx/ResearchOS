# Keyword Extraction Notes

The extractor is designed for the first paper in a search session, so abstract understanding is more important than raw keyword volume.

## Abstract First

Prefer the abstract over full text for early search expansion. Summarize:

- Background: research problem, gap, objective, or motivation.
- Methods: design, data, model, experiment, intervention, measurement, or analysis.
- Results: key findings, comparisons, effects, associations, or performance.
- Conclusion: final implication when present.

## Good Keywords

- Domain nouns and noun phrases.
- Topic, population, system, material, organism, disease, method, model, metric, intervention, mechanism, or outcome names.
- Phrases that are specific enough to narrow a search.
- Terms that appear in title, abstract, section headings, or repeated result sentences.

## Weak Keywords

- Generic scholarly words: study, result, method, analysis, significant, paper.
- Publisher or document furniture: copyright, license, figure, table, supplementary.
- Single-letter variables and short fragments.

## Search Query Shape

Prefer queries that combine:

- The original user query when known.
- 1-2 extracted phrases from background, methods, or results.
- A concrete method/result term rather than generic scholarly words.
