# Domain Entity Taxonomy

## Biology

- Disease: cancer, carcinoma, infection, inflammatory disease, metabolic disease.
- Gene/protein: named genes, proteins, receptors, enzymes, cytokines, transcription factors.
- Pathway: PI3K/AKT, MAPK, NF-kB, Wnt, mTOR, p53, apoptosis, autophagy.
- Cell line: HeLa, MCF-7, A549, HEK293, RAW264.7, HCT116, HepG2 and project-specific cell models.
- Assay: qPCR, Western blot, ELISA, MTT, CCK-8, flow cytometry, RNA-seq.

## Chemistry

- Compound: named chemical, formula-like string, reagent, product, ligand.
- Solvent: water, ethanol, methanol, DMSO, DMF, acetone, acetonitrile, toluene.
- Catalyst: metal catalyst, enzyme catalyst, acid/base catalyst, photocatalyst.
- Reaction: oxidation, reduction, coupling, substitution, polymerization, hydrolysis.
- Condition: temperature, time, pH, concentration, atmosphere, light.

## Materials

- Material: perovskite, MOF, COF, polymer, alloy, oxide, sulfide, graphene, nanoparticle.
- Synthesis condition: annealing, calcination, solvothermal, hydrothermal, spin coating.
- Characterization: XRD, SEM, TEM, FTIR, Raman, XPS, BET, DSC, TGA.
- Performance: efficiency, conductivity, capacity, stability, selectivity, yield, tensile strength.

## Boundary

Heuristic extraction is not authoritative normalization. Treat extracted entities as candidates until linked to curated databases or article evidence.
