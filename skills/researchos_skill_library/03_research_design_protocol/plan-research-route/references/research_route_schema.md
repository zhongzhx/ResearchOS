# Research Route Schema

Use this schema when producing a structured research route.

## Route Sections

1. Project framing
   - Topic
   - Field
   - Degree level
   - Target output
   - Duration
   - Known constraints

2. Scope boundary
   - In scope
   - Out of scope
   - Required validation

3. Research-question candidates
   - Conservative route: easiest to execute, lower novelty
   - Differentiated route: balanced feasibility and novelty
   - Ambitious route: higher novelty, higher risk

4. Literature route
   - Seed keywords
   - Search blocks
   - Databases
   - Screening rules
   - Reading order
   - Review matrix columns

5. Method route
   - Data/materials
   - Primary method
   - Baseline/comparison
   - Validation plan
   - Analysis plan
   - Backup route

6. Schedule
   - Week 1
   - Week 2
   - Week 3
   - Week 4
   - Monthly milestones
   - Advisor checkpoints

7. Risk register
   - Risk
   - Signal
   - Mitigation
   - Decision point

8. Deliverables
   - Literature matrix
   - Research proposal
   - Experiment/reproduction plan
   - Interim report
   - Final thesis/paper output

## Quality Bar

- The route must be executable in the stated duration.
- Each method step must have an input, operation, and expected output.
- Each risky assumption must have a validation action.
- The one-page advisor summary must be discussion-ready.
- The route must not include fake citations or unsupported claims.
